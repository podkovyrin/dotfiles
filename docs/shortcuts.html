Herdr prefix in Ghostty: Cmd+S, release, then press the next key. Ctrl+B also works.

Configured shortcuts, not macOS or GUI app defaults. Herdr defaults are included because they complete the Cmd+S layer.

Behavior and scope
  • Caps+Shift is Hyper plus Shift. OmniWM's Hyper does not include Shift by itself.
  • OmniWM actions depend on the workspace layout. Column and span actions are most useful in Niri; the default layout is Dwindle.
  • Herdr's Ctrl+H/J/K/L replace the default prefix-based pane focus bindings. Option+1 through 9 select tabs; Cmd+1 through 9 select workspaces; Ctrl+1 through 9 focus agents.
  • Ghostty forwards Cmd+0 but Herdr has no configured workspace-0 binding. Cmd+T and Cmd+Shift+D do nothing.
  • Cmd+W sends Ctrl+D to the foreground terminal app. It does not unconditionally close a pane. At an empty shell prompt it normally exits the shell.
  • Zsh uses vi mode. Shell bindings apply at a shell prompt, unless Herdr intercepts them. Option is Alt in Ghostty. Shell entries summarize dotkit's custom widgets and fzf integration, not every standard editing key.
  • Pi's Option+N opens the custom prompt snippet menu. Libedit's Ctrl+R searches history in programs that read ~/.editrc.
  • Karabiner disables Cmd+H and Cmd+Shift+H globally. Caps+H still focuses the window to the left.
  • OmniWM also configures Option as its mouse move/resize modifier and Option+Shift as its scroll modifier. Workspace swipe, window move/resize gestures, and overview gestures are disabled.
  • Ghostty copies selected text to the clipboard. No extra copy shortcut is required.
